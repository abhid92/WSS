THE WSS EXPERIENCE

Soul Bazaar

India's largest marketplace for spirituality. Brands, ashrams, apps, and start-ups under one roof. Sub-items: Wellness Products (organic incense, malas, meditation cushions, sacred textiles, essential oils), Retreat Centers (yoga ashrams, vipassana centers, healing sanctuaries), Spiritual Tech (meditation apps, biofeedback devices, consciousness tracking platforms), Conscious Fashion (ethical clothing, handwoven fabrics, sustainable accessories).

THE WSS EXPERIENCE

Spiritual Arts and Culture Expo

Public festival zone with 200+ curated stalls and immersive rooms. Marketplace Zone: curated vendor stalls, product demonstrations, book signings and launches. Experience Zone: sound healing rooms, meditation pods, live art demonstrations.

Chamber of Oneness

A collective moment of unity culminating in Swami Vivekananda's Chicago speech recreation. Holographic Vivekananda delivers "Sisters and Brothers of America" — a powerful reminder that all religions lead to the same truth.
